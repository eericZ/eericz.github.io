Why ads

Ads are awesome!

  • They allow unknown products to be noticed.
  • They bond us together with their catchy song and jingles. Thank you Gen AI for those amazing videos and music!

  • They fill places and time that are otherwise wasted

The problem of ads

User engagement

Unfortunatly, there is a big flaw with ads. People may not be watching them. This makes the user not click on the ad after it finishes.
For improved ad engagement, we can ask the user what the ads was about before returning to the original content. Here are a list of possible questions:
  • What was the product name?
  • What is it used for?
  • What was the object on the background at 23s?
This solution is flawed, it breaks the natural flow of things: user arrives on the page -> user watches an ad -> user accesses the content.
One solution can be the use of an eye tracking software to determine if the user is actually watching the ad. There are open source solutions like WebGazer. If we are sure the user was indeed watching the whole ad, we can do them a favour and skip the questions, otherwise we are forced to ask the questions.
By implementing this option, we can reassure the ad space buyer that the user is engaged with the ad and thus is more likely to interact with it. That directly leads to increased price of the ad, and sucessful sell for them.

No internet conenction

By cutting the device from the internet, we won't be able to load ads for the user. There are a few ways to prevent user from putting the phone on airplane mode.
The simplest way is that the app legitimally required a stable internet connection to work.
Application that don't require an internet connection may want to have a mandatory account feature. By being offline for too long, the app should block the access to it and require an internet connection just to ensure the user is still who they claim to be. Durign this time, we can download a stock of ads we can show if the user goes offline again. These will be classified as offline ads. Prints will be locally counted with our extremly certified partner to ensure we are not over charging our advertisers, so they don't have to worry about that. They are cheaper that standard ads because they can be outdated and true count of the ad are always uncertain.

User don't like ads

We understand that sometimes, a user may feel there is too much ads. This is because ads company are not doing enough to make it work.
In this day and age, ads are required in everyone's daily life. It allows previously unknown product to become the next hit, it creates emotions and allows innovation. It promotes the consumption required to drive the economy. Ads companies should educate the masses on the importance of ads on our society
We are entering a transition age between a few static ads and high engagement ads. Today ads are not considered as normal but intrusive. This is because ads company are doing it too fast. Yet, we still need to acknowledge their successes; today, it is normal to see 2 unskippable ads before a video and every 2 to 3 minutes whareas a few years prior, seing an add banner is outrageous. The current status of ads is too much focused on the internet and it is starting to be visible by the populus. We should start by adding ads in more and more places and rebalance the ads space in order to avoid this localized spike of ads that we call the internet. That leads perfectly to out next section.

Where to put ads?

There are limitless places to put ads, you just have to find it. Here is some places I found just for you!

In cities

Walls

On buildings and houses: sell a bland, featureless, sad looking wall as advertising space and make profit, or if you are the owner of a appartment complex, lower the rent! Happy people everywhere!

Benches and seats

Add some reinforced spikes on the seat and a button to show an ad. After the ad ends, the user can seat for 10 minutes. One ad per 10 minutes is nothing, right? When people are in need we help them.
Same reasonning for restaurant's seats, customers are paying for the food the owner must buy, they pay a staff tip for the cook, they give additional tip so the owner don't have to pay the server as much. We are forgetting the place they take that are often free. By requiring them to watch ads beforehand, we are monetizing the seats. This also have the added benefit to encourege the customer to not linger and hold the place that should be freed for other customers.

Hospital and healthcare center

Those are the people the most in need, they are looking for solutions. We are not targeting them, they are looking for help and we can help. The good thing is that those kind of places have a lot of qualified personels that can explain them the benefit of the product in an ad and also give nuance when the customer asks for it. They are also able to contact the seller and give them advice or explain why some information can be wrong. This is a win win situation.

Ground

People need to look where they walk, and where to they walk? You guessed it, the ground. Having ads on the ground is not as intrusive as pre-roll or flashy screen Yet, even static images can be engaging, smart advertisers can print little games to play for the whole walk and keep the user hooked while they are doing something as mindless as walking.
We are very convinced that we should not pollute our beautiful planet, that why we are using a 100% biodegradable non toxic paint. Use the ground as a giant canvas for all to see, even from the sky!

Car

Car paint

Buy a new car for less if you agree to put some ads on you car. Every month, go back to the car dealership to change the ad and pocket a lump of cash to pay for the car loan. Why would you keep a bleak monotone colour when you can pay less, get payed and have a colourful car?

Temporarly unlock premium features

Some people may not want to pay extra for premium features like air conditioning, GPS, or car radio. But, they may need it in a pinch. So we may gracefully provide this service for just a few ads. They have not payed for the service so they should expect nothing from us. We are giving them a favour by allowing to use VIP features.
Of course, we are sensible people, we will not show ads when they are driving, they have to stop their car first.

## The problem of ads

### User engagement

Unfortunatly, there is a big flaw with ads. People may not be watching them. This makes the user not click on the ad after it finishes.

For improved ad engagement, we can ask the user what the ads was about before returning to the original content. Here are a list of possible questions:
    
 - What was the product name?
 - What is it used for?
 - What was the object on the background at 23s?

This solution is flawed, it breaks the natural flow of things: user arrives on the page -> user watches an ad -> user accesses the content.

One solution can be the use of an eye tracking software to determine if the user is actually watching the ad. There
are open source solutions like [WebGazer](https://webgazer.cs.brown.edu/)
If we are sure the user was indeed watching the whole ad, we can do them a favour and skip the questions, otherwise we are forced to ask the questions.

By implementing this option, we can reassure the ad space buyer that the user is engaged with the ad and thus is more likely to interact with it. That directly leads to increased price of the ad, and sucessful sell for them.

### User don't like ads

We understand that sometimes, a user may feel there is too much ads. This is because ads company are not doing enough to make it work.
In this day and age, ads are required in everyone's daily life. It allows previously unknown product to become the next hit, it creates emotions and allows innovation. It promotes the consumption required to drive the economy. Ads companies should educate the masses on the importance of ads on our society. We are entering a transition age between a few static ads and high engagement ads. Today ads are not considered as normal but intrusive. This is because ads company are doing it too fast. Yet, we still need to acknowledge their successes; today, it is normal to see 2 unskippable ads before a video and every 2 to 3 minutes whareas a few years prior, seing an add banner is outrageous. The current status of ads is too much focused on the internet and it is starting to be visible by the populus.
